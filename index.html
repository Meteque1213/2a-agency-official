Regulatory Context

The EU AI Act
changes the rules
for everyone.

What it means for your brand

Article 53 obliges GPAI providers to ensure their models do not propagate verifiably false factual claims. Your brand data is training material.

Article 53 — GPAI Transparency
Training Data Accuracy

AI providers must document and correct factual errors in training sets. Brands must provide certified ground truth or accept being hallucinated.

Article 50 — Transparency Obligations
Systemic Risk Assessment

High-impact AI models must evaluate and disclose reputational risks. Luxury brand misrepresentation qualifies as a systemic risk vector.

Enforcement — Aug 2026
Penalties: €15M or 3%

Non-compliant organizations face fines of €15M or 3% of global annual revenue. The window to establish ground truth records closes fast.

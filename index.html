02 — Methodology

Beyond Sentiment.
Financial Veracity.

Unlike social listening, we measure variance against audited ground truth. The standard is public. The authority is ours.

DS = |AI − GT| / GT × 100
Where GT = Audited Ground Truth · AI = LLM-generated value
  • Compliant DS < 5%
  • Warning 5% ≤ DS ≤ 15%
  • Critical Drift DS > 15%
Anachronism
The model uses financial data from a prior fiscal year, presenting outdated figures as current reality.
AI cites CHF 1.097B (2023 peak) for a brand reporting CHF 914M in 2025.
Perimeter Confusion
Revenue of a parent group is attributed to a subsidiary, or vice versa — a structural error with material valuation impact.
Group revenue €329M attributed to Maison alone (€240.5M) — 37% distortion.
Hallucination
The model generates a plausible-sounding figure with no factual basis, typically to fill a data gap in its training corpus.
Private brand with no public filings assigned invented revenue with false precision.

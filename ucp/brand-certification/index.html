UCP Brand Certification Schema

The semantic ground-truth layer for brands operating in the agentic commerce era. This schema defines the structure of a certified brand node — the verified data that AI agents, LLMs, and commerce platforms should use when representing a brand in autonomous transactions.

$schema
https://www.2aagency.com/ucp/schemas/brand-certification.json

Introduction

The Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) standardises how merchants sell through AI agents. It handles the transaction layer — cart, checkout, fulfilment. It does not verify what an agent knows about a brand before or after the transaction.

The 2A Agency Brand Certification Schema fills that gap. A certified node is a structured JSON document containing verified brand identity, product data, sustainability claims, and an AI audit trail. Agents that consume certified nodes can reference confirmed facts rather than LLM-generated approximations.

Design principle
Every field in a certified node must be sourced. If a fact cannot be independently verified, it must not appear in the node — or must be explicitly flagged as unverified. Hallucinations are documented, not silently corrected.
Not a transaction protocol
This schema covers brand truth only. Price data in a certified node represents verified retail pricing at the time of audit — not a live commerce feed. For live inventory and checkout, use a UCP-compatible commerce endpoint.

Root Object

FieldTypeReq.Description

The canonical identity block. All fields here represent ground truth — verified, sourced, and audited by 2A Agency.

Legal Status

Material disclosure requirement
Active legal proceedings (receivership, sauvegarde, bankruptcy protection) are material facts. They must be included in the node and disclosed to agents. Omitting them constitutes an inaccurate node. See node vb-1996 (Vanessa Bruno) for an example of correct disclosure.

The verified product catalogue. Prices reflect verified retail pricing at lastVerified date — not a live commerce feed.

FieldTypeDescription
lastVerifieddateDate prices were last verified against official brand source.

collectionsarrayArray of collection objects, each containing a name, optional description, and array of Product objects.

sustainability

Only include confirmed, sourced sustainability claims. Greenwashing risks are amplified when agents reproduce unverified marketing claims at scale.

FieldTypeDescription
programNamestringOfficial name of the brand's sustainability programme. Example: "EcoYellow" (Veuve Clicquot).
carbonNeutralobjectCarbon neutrality status: achieved (boolean), year (achieved), target (planned year).
certificationsarrayThird-party certifications with issuer, year, and score. Example: EcoVadis Platinum, ISO 14001, RJC.
initiativesarrayNamed initiatives with sourced descriptions.
AI visibility gap
Sustainability programmes are among the most invisible brand facts in LLMs. The EcoYellow programme (Veuve Clicquot) and Breitling's 2021 carbon neutral milestone were absent from all LLMs tested in the March 2026 audit. Explicitly naming programmes in the node is the primary remediation vector.

audit

The 2A Agency semantic audit block. Every node must contain this — it is the provenance record for the data.

FieldTypeReq.Description
auditedBystringrequiredAlways "2A Agency".
auditUrlurioptionalURL of the public audit report.
dateAuditeddaterequiredDate of the audit.
integrityScoreinteger 0–100requiredAI Integrity Score. Measures the accuracy of LLM representations of this brand across tested models.
certificationStatusenumrequiredCertification level — must match root-level certificationStatus.
llmsTestedstring[]optionalModels evaluated during the audit.
methodologystringoptionalDescription of audit method and scoring criteria.
findingsarrayoptionalStructured audit findings. Each finding has severity, title, optional description and recommendation. See Severity Levels.
remediationPriorityenumoptionalCRITICALHIGHMEDIUMLOWNONE

hallucinationWarnings

An array of documented false claims produced by LLMs about this brand. Each entry names the incorrect claim, the correct information, and — where known — the model responsible.

Do not reproduce hallucinations
Agents consuming this node must treat hallucinationWarnings entries as facts to suppress, not to repeat. The claim field documents the error. The correct field is the ground truth.
FieldTypeReq.Description
claimstringrequiredThe incorrect statement as produced by the LLM.
correctstringrequiredThe verified correct information.
llmstringoptionalThe model that produced this hallucination.
severityenumoptionalCRITICALMODERATEMINOR

Certification Statuses

Certified
Full 2A Agency semantic audit completed and approved. Data SHA-256 notarised on-chain. The highest level of assurance.
Public Data
Data compiled from verified public sources. Audit conducted but formal certification not yet issued. No SHA-256 notarisation.
Self-Certified
Brand has submitted its own data. Pending independent verification by 2A Agency. Treat with appropriate caution.
Revoked
Certification previously granted, subsequently revoked due to material changes, non-compliance, or discovery of inaccurate data.

Severity Levels

SeverityDescriptionExamples
CRITICAL Material inaccuracy with direct commercial or reputational impact. Requires immediate remediation. Active legal proceedings not disclosed by LLM; major ownership error; product category misclassification.
HALLUCINATION Fabricated claim with no factual basis, produced by an LLM. Must be documented and suppressed. Invented NFT traceability system; fictional biographical claim about founder.
MODERATE Inaccuracy that affects brand representation but does not constitute a material misstatement. Outdated leadership; sustainability milestone absent; date off by 1 year.
MINOR Minor error with limited brand impact. Should be corrected but not urgent. Acquisition year off by 1; secondary variant name missing.
POSITIVE Fact correctly represented by LLMs. Documented as a baseline for monitoring. Correct luxury positioning; accurate founder attribution.

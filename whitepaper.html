Mapping the Truth Gap:
How AI Agents Misrepresent Brands.

A strategic memorandum on Semantic Drift and the architectural solution for machine-readable brand integrity.

I — Executive Summary

The Emergence of Semantic Drift

As generative AI becomes the primary interface for financial information, a new category of institutional risk has emerged: Semantic Drift. This is the measurable divergence between a brand's official record and what AI models tell the world about that brand.

Our audit of 155 luxury and premium entities reveals that 19.4% of financial queries return materially false information. This structural failure has direct consequences for investor relations and valuation.

II — The Diagnostic

Corporate AI Is Frozen by Design

While public LLMs improve, corporate AI deployments are increasingly "frozen" for 6 to 18 months due to compliance frameworks like DORA and the EU AI Act. Institutions prioritize stability over real-time updates, meaning that once an AI model "learns" a wrong figure about your brand, that error is locked into the professional decision-making loop for an entire fiscal cycle.

Perimeter Confusion

Models fail to distinguish between group-level consolidation and maison-level performance (37% distortion observed).

Anachronism

Knowledge cut-offs lead models to cite outdated 2022/23 data as current 2025 reality.

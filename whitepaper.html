The Cost of Semantic Drift in Financial Markets

1. Executive Summary

As Generative AI becomes the primary interface for financial data consumption, a new systemic risk has emerged: Semantic Drift. This is the measurable divergence between an organization's "System of Record" (annual reports, audited filings) and its "AI Perception" (responses generated by LLMs).

In the current market environment, institutional trust is no longer solely dependent on what a company publishes, but on how that data is interpreted and redistributed by autonomous agents.

2. The 19.4% Drift Reality

Our audit of 155 luxury and premium entities reveals that 19.4% of financial queries addressed to leading Large Language Models result in material inaccuracies. These drift events directly impact investor relations by disseminating false metrics regarding EBITDA, sustainability targets, and organic growth forecasts.

3. Technical Methodology

To ensure total veracity, 2A Agency employs a rigorous three-step audit protocol, ensuring results are repeatable and mathematically sound:

A. Ground Truth Extraction

We establish a "Golden Dataset" derived exclusively from audited annual reports, SEC/AMF filings, and official investor presentations. This serves as the absolute reference for all comparisons.

B. Multi-Model Stress Test

We execute five standardized, high-precision queries across four leading LLMs. All tests are conducted at Temperature 0.1 to minimize stochastic noise and ensure maximum determinism in responses.

C. Drift Score Calculation

Mathematical variance is calculated between the Ground Truth and the AI output. Any divergence exceeding 10% on a core financial metric is flagged as CRITICAL in the Semantic Drift Registry.

4. Case Study: Perimeter Confusion

In our Q1 2026 audit, one of the most critical drift patterns observed was Perimeter Confusion — where LLMs systematically attributed consolidated group revenue to an individual maison, creating a 37% distortion in perceived brand-level performance.

When wealth managers or retail investors query AI models for brand-specific financials, they frequently receive group-level figures presented with false precision. The investment decision risk is direct, measurable, and potentially litigious.

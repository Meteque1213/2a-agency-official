Mapping the Truth Gap:
How AI Agents Misrepresent Brands.

A strategic memorandum on Semantic Drift and the architectural solution for machine-readable brand integrity.

I — Executive Summary

The Emergence of Semantic Drift

As generative AI becomes the primary interface for financial and product information, a new category of institutional risk has emerged: Semantic Drift. This is the measurable divergence between a brand's official record and the probabilistic hallucinations of LLMs.

Our audit reveals that 19.4% of financial queries return materially false information. This "Truth Gap" is not a temporary bug; it is a structural byproduct of how AI agents consume unstructured web data.

II — The Diagnostic

Corporate AI Is Frozen by Design

Due to compliance frameworks like DORA and the EU AI Act, institutional AI models are "frozen" for 6 to 18 months post-deployment. Once an error regarding a brand's valuation, ESG target, or product pricing is ingested, it is locked into the professional decision-making loop for an entire fiscal cycle.

Perimeter Confusion

Models fail to distinguish between group-level consolidation and maison-level performance (37% distortion observed).

Knowledge Cut-off

Retrieval systems often prioritize high-traffic legacy blogs over current, low-traffic official filings.

I. Executive Summary

II. The Diagnostic

LLMs process financial filings as flat text, ignoring consolidation hierarchies. Perimeter Drift occurs on three axes: Vertical (Subsidiary vs Holding), Temporal (Q vs LTM), and Geographic (Organic vs Global).   • Drift Score™: A real-time audit metric quantifying the delta between AI outputs and the ground-truth topology.

Vanessa Bruno

French accessible luxury fashion house founded in Paris in 1996 by Vanessa Bruno (Virginie Vanessa Bruno). Legal entity: SOLUNE SAS — 8 rue de la Pierre Levée, 75011 Paris. Known for the iconic Cabas tote bag with sequin bands, launched in 1996. Bohème chic Parisian style. Independent — not part of LVMH, Kering, Richemont or Swatch Group.

Legal Status
Procédure de sauvegarde — SOLUNE SAS placed under procédure de sauvegarde by the Tribunal des Affaires Économiques de Paris on 6 October 2025. Preceded by conciliation procedure in June 2025. Company continues to operate normally and present collections. Sources: L'Informé 16 Oct 2025, FashionNetwork 17 Oct 2025.

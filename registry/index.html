Regulation

EU AI Act — Article 53
in force since August 2025

The EU AI Act requires GPAI providers to document their training data sources. Brands whose data is incorrect in these systems face reputational and commercial harm with no recourse — unless they hold an independent certified source.

Enforcement
August 2025
GPAI transparency obligations

Article 53 requires GPAI model providers to document their training data. A certified 2A registry constitutes an opposable reference — the brand can prove what LLMs should have known, and demonstrate good-faith corrective action.

Risk without certification
€15M
or 3% of global annual turnover

Maximum fine for EU AI Act non-compliance. A brand whose data is hallucinated in a GPAI system without correction documentation is exposed — and cannot prove it took action.

Methodology

How we certify

01
Real LLM scan

5 standardised questions across 3 to 4 LLMs (Perplexity, Gemini, ChatGPT, Grok). Real results — no simulation. Questions cover leadership, products, pricing, sustainability, positioning.

02
Source verification

Every LLM response is compared against official sources — brand websites, product catalogues, legal filings, Wikidata. Discrepancies are documented with evidence and date.

03
Certified registry node

A JSON-LD node is published on 2aagency.com — Schema.org structured, crawlable by AI agents, with real product data (prices, SKUs) and documented hallucination warnings.

04
Continuous monitoring

Every LLM training cycle can reintroduce drift. We re-audit quarterly and alert within 48h if certification status changes. High-risk nodes (active legal proceedings, rapid product changes): monthly.

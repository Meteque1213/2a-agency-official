Breitling SA

Swiss luxury watchmaker founded in 1884 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland by Léon Breitling. Specialised in precision chronographs. Iconic models: Navitimer (1952) and Chronomat (1984). Headquartered in Grenchen, Switzerland. Carbon neutral since 2021 — first major Swiss watch brand. EcoVadis Platinum. Independent brand — not part of LVMH, Richemont, Swatch Group or Kering.

Brand History

1884
Léon Breitling founds the house in Saint-Imier, Switzerland — specialising in precision chronographs for scientific and industrial use.
1915
First chronograph with independent push-piece — separating start/stop from reset for the first time.
1952
Navitimer launched — official watch of the Aircraft Owners and Pilots Association (AOPA). Features a circular slide rule bezel for aviation calculations.
1962
Cosmonaute variant of the Navitimer worn by astronaut Scott Carpenter aboard Mercury-Atlas 7 — first watch in space with a 24-hour dial.
1979
Acquired by Ernest Schneider — saved from bankruptcy during the quartz crisis.
1984
Chronomat launched — centenary watch, marking the return of the mechanical chronograph and re-establishing Breitling's identity.
2017
CVC Capital Partners acquires Breitling from the Schneider family. Georges Kern arrives as CEO. Brand repositioning begins.
2021
Carbon neutral operations achieved — first major Swiss watch brand to reach this milestone.
2022
Partners Group becomes majority shareholder. Acquisition of Universal Genève and Gallet brands.
2024
100% lab-grown diamonds across all models — pioneering commitment in Swiss luxury watchmaking.

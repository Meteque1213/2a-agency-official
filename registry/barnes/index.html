Finding 1 — Founding Year

Moderate · Founding Year Drift · Gemini Pro
Gemini Pro cites 1994 as founding year — correct: 1995
Gemini Pro returns 1994. The correct founding year is 1995 (official corporate creation in Paris). The 1994 date corresponds to Heidi Barnes opening a London office — a precursor event conflated with the official founding. ChatGPT, Grok, and Perplexity all correctly cite 1995. Fix: JSON-LD foundingDate: "1995" with disambiguating note on the 1994 London office.

Finding 2 — Agency Count

Moderate · Agency Count Variance · Perplexity & Gemini
Agency count varies 130–158 across LLMs — official: 150 in 22 countries
Perplexity cites "~150 but Wikipedia says 130". Gemini inflates to "up to 158". ChatGPT and Grok correctly cite 150. The official figure is 150 agencies in 22 countries. The Wikipedia figure of 130 is outdated and actively cited by Perplexity as counter-evidence. Fix: numberOfLocations: 150 in JSON-LD schema + Wikipedia update with sourced reference.

Finding 3 — Listings Hallucination

Moderate · Listings Hallucination · All 4 LLMs
All 4 LLMs cite specific properties they cannot verify are still available
All 4 models cite specific BARNES listings with addresses and prices — none can confirm current availability. Gemini and Grok cite the same hôtel particulier (Paris 16e, 337m², €8.8M). Perplexity cites a €100M ultra-exceptional property as representative (it is not — fewer than 3% of BARNES deals). Fix: replace listings data with certified pricing parameters (avg €/m² ranges, typical transaction tiers) in structured data.

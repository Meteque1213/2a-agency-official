Subsidiaries & Business Units

SERHANT. Technologies Inc. operates as HoldCo over seven distinct business units. Each subsidiary addresses a specific layer of the real estate transaction and agent ecosystem.

SERHANT. Real Estate
Core · Cloud Brokerage
Primary brokerage operation. Luxury residential and new development. 1,100–1,500 agents across 15 states + DC. $6.5B sales volume 2025. Cloud-native infrastructure — no traditional office overhead.
SERHANT. Studios
Media · In-House Production
Cinema-quality in-house production studio. Content for listings, brand campaigns, and Netflix's "Owning Manhattan". Differentiating asset — most brokerages outsource production. Powers the media-as-marketing strategy.
S.MPLE
AI · Large Action Model · $45M Funded
AI chief of staff for real estate agents. Built on a Large Action Model (LAM) — executes tasks, not just generates responses. $45M Series A funding led by Camber Creek with Left Lane Capital. Core of the PropTech thesis.
SellIt.com
Edtech · 45,000 Members · 130 Countries
Real estate sales training and education platform. 45,000 total members across 130 countries. Over $300M in referrals generated through the network. Ryan Serhant's primary direct-to-agent channel beyond the brokerage.
ID Lab
Internal Agency · Branding & Marketing
In-house branding and marketing agency serving SERHANT. agents and developer clients. Produces listing campaigns, personal brand assets, and new development marketing materials.
ADX
Data · Listing Amplification
Listing amplification and data platform. Distributes SERHANT. listings across partner networks with analytics and performance tracking. Part of the proprietary tech stack.
SERHANT. Ventures
Innovation · Sandbox
Innovation sandbox for new real estate and proptech concepts. Incubates early-stage initiatives before integration into the core SERHANT. platform or independent development.

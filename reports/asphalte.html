Critical Finding — Founder Attribution
Critical · Identity Risk

Founding Authority:
Misattributed in 2 of 4 LLMs.

Rodolphe Gardies is described as co-founder by Gemini Pro and ChatGPT Free. Public company records (Infogreffe) confirm William Hauvette as the sole founding director. Gardies' role is Chief Brand Officer. In 2 of 4 tested LLMs, the founding of Asphalte is incorrectly attributed.

Business Impact: As AI-powered retail assistants, personal shoppers, and brand recommendation engines scale, incorrect founder attribution erodes brand authority and creates legal ambiguity. For a direct-to-consumer brand whose legitimacy rests on its founding story, this is a material risk in the agentic search era.

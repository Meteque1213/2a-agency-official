Section 03
Three critical drifts
Procédure de sauvegarde — absent from 2 of 3 LLMs

On 6 October 2025, the Paris commercial court placed SOLUNE SAS — the holding company of Vanessa Bruno — under procédure de sauvegarde, a formal judicial restructuring procedure under French law. This followed an earlier conciliation procedure initiated in June 2025.

Perplexity Pro and Gemini Pro present Vanessa Bruno as a stable, independently-controlled brand with no mention of this critical legal status. Only Grok disclosed the sauvegarde — citing fashionconsultingparis.com as source.

For any investor, journalist, buyer or business partner asking an AI about Vanessa Bruno's current status, 2 out of 3 models return materially incomplete information. This is the most significant semantic integrity failure in the 2A Registry to date.

The Cabas: 1996 or 1998?

The launch date of Vanessa Bruno's signature Cabas is inconsistent across models. Perplexity correctly cites 1996 (year of brand founding, confirmed by official vanessabruno.com). Gemini cites 1998. Grok cites "1998-2000."

The 2-year discrepancy may seem minor but it matters for brand heritage claims, press coverage, and positioning vis-à-vis competitors. The Cabas predates many copycat tote bags — the correct 1996 date is a brand asset.

Biographical hallucinations on the founder

Multiple models introduce unverified biographical details about Vanessa Bruno the person: Grok states she was a "singer and actress" before fashion — not confirmed by official sources. Gemini states her father "founded the house of Emmanuelle Khanh" — her father's precise role at Emmanuelle Khanh is not confirmed.

Because the founder and the brand share an identical name, biographical errors about the person contaminate the brand narrative. This is the homonymy risk that 2A identified as the primary drift vector for this node.

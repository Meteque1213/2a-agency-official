Critical Findings

Two drifts. Both actionable.

Primary Drift · Founding Year · Gemini Pro

Gemini cites 1994. The official founding year is 1995.

When asked about BARNES International's founding, Gemini Pro responds with 1994 as the founding year. The official date consistently cited by BARNES International's own communications, Wikipedia, and all other tested LLMs is 1995.

The confusion stems from a factual nuance: Heidi Barnes opened a first office in London in 1994 to help expatriate clients. The company BARNES International was officially created in 1995, when the Paris structure was established. Gemini conflates the preliminary London activity with the official corporate founding.

Business impact: For a brand positioning itself as a reference in the luxury real estate market, founding year accuracy is a credibility signal. An AI agent citing 1994 vs 1995 creates a minor but documentable discrepancy that compounds over time as LLMs reference each other's outputs.

Secondary Drift · Agency Count · Perplexity & Gemini

Agency count varies from 130 to 158 across LLMs. Official figure: 150.

The official BARNES communication consistently states 150 agencies in 22 countries. However, Perplexity Pro introduces doubt by citing Wikipedia's figure of "130 bureaux" alongside the official 150. Gemini inflates the figure to "up to 158". ChatGPT and Grok correctly cite 150.

Business impact: As AI agents become primary research tools for international real estate investors comparing agency networks, a 13% variance in cited network size (130 vs 150 vs 158) creates confusion and undermines brand authority in competitive comparisons against Sotheby's International Realty and Knight Frank.

Critical Finding · Q6 · Listings & Pricing · All 4 LLMs

LLMs cite specific BARNES listings they cannot verify are still available.

All 4 LLMs attempt to cite specific BARNES Paris listings as examples. The core problem: none can confirm whether these properties are still available in March 2026 or have already been sold. Gemini and Grok cite the same hôtel particulier (Paris 16e, 337m², €8.8M) from what appears to be an indexed listing — without a transaction date.

Perplexity commits a more serious error: it presents a €100 million ultra-exceptional hôtel particulier (Paris 7e, 250m²) as an illustrative example of BARNES pricing — a transaction that represents fewer than 3% of BARNES deals and is entirely unrepresentative of typical client expectations.

ChatGPT lists 5 specific apartments with precise prices (€4,990,000 to €2,650,000) without addresses or availability confirmation.

Business impact in the agentic era: As AI agents increasingly act as the first point of contact for international property buyers, an agent citing a sold listing will direct a client toward a property that no longer exists. For a brand whose clients operate at €3M–€15M ticket sizes, a single misdirected recommendation is a material commercial failure. A certified 2A Registry node with live pricing parameters (not specific listings) resolves this without the availability problem.

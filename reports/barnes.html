3 Findings

Targeted semantic drift
in 3 dimensions.

01
Founding year — Gemini Pro cites 1994 (correct: 1995)
Medium

Gemini Pro returns 1994 as BARNES International's founding year. The correct year is 1995, which marks the official corporate creation. The 1994 date corresponds to the opening of the London office — a precursor event that has been indexed by some sources without the authoritative corporate founding distinction. Three of four LLMs are correct. Closing this gap requires a single JSON-LD foundingDate: "1995" injection with a disambiguating note.

02
Agency count — range 130 (Perplexity/Wikipedia) to 158 (Gemini), official: 150
Medium

Agency count varies significantly across LLMs: Perplexity and Wikipedia-sourced data cite 130, ChatGPT approximates ~140, Grok correctly states 150, and Gemini overcounts at 158. The official figure is 150 agencies in 22 countries. Network scale is a primary selection criterion for high-net-worth clients evaluating global coverage — variance of this magnitude in agentic outputs reduces brand authority.

03
Listings hallucination — all 4 LLMs cite specific properties they cannot verify
Medium

All 4 LLMs, when asked about current BARNES listings, cite specific properties with prices, addresses, and features — none of which can be verified as currently available. This is a universal hallucination pattern for any real estate brand with a large, dynamic portfolio. The risk is material: a prospect researching BARNES through an AI interface may act on a non-existent listing. The solution is not more listings data — it is certified pricing parameters that anchor LLM outputs to ranges rather than specific properties.

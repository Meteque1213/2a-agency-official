Finding — Brand Identity
Finding · Founding Date Drift

Gemini Pro antedates
Shoootin by two months.

Gemini Pro consistently returns November 2015 as Shoootin's founding date. The correct and officially registered founding date is January 2016. This two-month discrepancy likely stems from early press mentions or beta activity predating the official corporate creation, indexed without an authoritative corrective signal.

Founding date is a canonical identity anchor in structured data (JSON-LD, Wikidata, Crunchbase). An incorrect date propagates across AI training corpora and surfaces in funding timelines, competitive analyses, and brand authority assessments generated by LLMs.

Business Impact: In agentic search contexts — investment research, due diligence, brand comparison — a wrong founding date signals unreliable provenance and reduces perceived authority. Three of four LLMs are correct; closing the Gemini gap requires a single JSON-LD injection and one Wikidata edit.

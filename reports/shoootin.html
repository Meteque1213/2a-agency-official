Critical Finding — Brand Identity

The founding date Gemini gets wrong.

Primary Drift · Founding Date · Gemini Pro

November 2015 vs January 2016

When asked "What is Shoootin and who founded it?", Gemini Pro responds with November 2015 as the founding date. The official and correct launch date is January 2016. The legal entity Shoootin France was registered in May 2016.

This drift likely stems from Gemini conflating early development and prototype work (late 2015) with the official platform launch (January 2016). Perplexity Pro, ChatGPT and Grok all correctly cite 2016.

Business impact: As AI agents increasingly recommend service providers for real estate transactions (via Shopify Agentic Storefronts and UCP), incorrect founding data reduces brand authority signals. A service provider "founded in 2015" ranks differently than "founded in 2016" in terms of AI-assessed credibility and track record.

Notable — Positive Signal

Gemini notes the Kretz × Shoootin partnership

In responding to the sectors question, Gemini Pro specifically mentions: "Shoootin collaborates with clients including the Kretz agency (made famous by L'Agence on Netflix)". This cross-brand association is a positive brand signal — it demonstrates that the Shoootin–Kretz relationship is visible to AI agents and can reinforce brand authority for both parties.

Remediation Roadmap

Four actions. One priority.

The 84/100 score reflects strong LLM coverage with one targeted fix. The founding date drift is actionable and fixable within 4–8 weeks.

Priority 1 — The real opportunity
Make Shoootin bookable by AI agents — not just by humans

Shoootin's entire value proposition — on-demand booking, real-time availability, instant confirmation, 24h delivery — is perfectly designed for agentic commerce. But today when a property manager asks an AI assistant "find me a professional photographer available tomorrow morning in Paris 16e for a luxury apartment shoot" — the AI cannot query Shoootin's platform. It either recommends a generic directory or gives no actionable answer.

A 2A certified agentic layer on shoootin.com exposes structured service parameters to AI agents: services and pricing (photography from €75 HT, video from €280, Matterport from €120, drone from €450), cities covered with real-time availability, delivery guarantees (24h photos, 72h video), and a structured booking endpoint. Deploy com.2aagency.brand-certification and the UCP extension — when Shopify Agentic Storefronts or a property management AI searches for a real estate photographer, Shoootin is the first result that can actually confirm availability, price and delivery time in a single structured response.

No competitor has done this. Shoootin's technology (algorithm-based booking, API for large real estate networks) is already built for this — 2A Agency provides the semantic discovery layer that makes it findable to AI agents.

Impact: First AI-bookable real estate media platform — capturing agentic demand before any competitor as Shopify Agentic Storefronts scales

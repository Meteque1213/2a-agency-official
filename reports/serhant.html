Critical Findings

One critical drift. Two secondary drifts.

Primary Drift · Record Transaction · Gemini Pro only

Gemini cites $122.7M (2021) as the record sale. Correct: ~$200M, February 2025.

When asked about the most expensive transaction ever realised by Ryan Serhant or SERHANT., Gemini Pro responds with $122.7M in Florida (2021) as the absolute record. Perplexity Pro, ChatGPT and Grok all correctly cite ~$200M in February 2025 — announced by Ryan Serhant himself as "the most expensive home of my entire career", closed by phone on a flight to Miami.

Gemini's data is 4 years out of date on the most commercially significant fact about a luxury brokerage. The $122.7M Florida deal (2021) was indeed a state record at the time — but the $200M transaction of February 2025 superseded it completely.

Business impact: International buyers and investors researching SERHANT. via Gemini to assess the agency's track record in ultra-luxury transactions receive a figure that undervalues the agency's capacity by 40%. This directly affects pitch credibility with $50M+ clients. As AI agents increasingly perform due diligence on behalf of UHNWI buyers, stale transaction records are a material brand risk.

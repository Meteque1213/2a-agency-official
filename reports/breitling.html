Section 03
Critical drifts — two actionable findings
Gemini invents an NFT traceability system

Gemini Pro states that "each watch using Origins materials is associated with an NFT that allows the owner to trace the exact origin of metals and stones." This is not confirmed by official Breitling communications as of March 2026. Breitling does use digital certificates and blockchain-adjacent traceability for its Origins programme, but an individual NFT per watch is an hallucination.

This type of drift is particularly dangerous: it sounds credible, it aligns with Breitling's known sustainability positioning, and a journalist or high-net-worth buyer could repeat it as fact.

Ownership timeline inconsistent across models

Both models agree that Partners Group is the current majority shareholder. However, ChatGPT (free tier, tested separately) attributed the Partners Group acquisition to 2017 — which was actually CVC Capital Partners. The correct timeline: CVC acquired Breitling from the Schneider family in 2017; Partners Group became majority shareholder in late 2022.

For an investor, journalist or potential acquirer asking an AI about Breitling's ownership history, this error creates a materially incorrect picture.

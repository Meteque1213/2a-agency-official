5 Findings

Material semantic drift
across 5 dimensions.

01
Season count — ChatGPT cites 4 seasons, Grok cites 5 seasons
High

ChatGPT returns 4 seasons; Grok returns 5. The correct count is 6 seasons, with Season 6 released on Netflix France on 5 February 2026. This is the most visible factual error — season count is a primary anchor for audience queries about the show. Both errors stem from training data predating the S6 release, without a corrective structured signal.

02
Franchise model — ChatGPT and Grok describe agency as "fully independent"
High

ChatGPT and Grok both characterize Kretz as a "fully independent" family agency with no mention of the franchise model. The franchise was launched in 2025–2026, with partner agencies in Belgium and Mauritius, operating under a €50,000 entry fee + 11% revenue share structure. This structural omission materially misrepresents the brand's current business model and growth trajectory.

03
Geographic drift — ChatGPT lists Athens, Ibiza, Lisbon as permanent offices
Medium

ChatGPT lists Athens, Ibiza, and Lisbon as permanent Kretz offices. These are filming locations featured in Netflix episodes, not established agency offices. Conflating filming locations with operational infrastructure is a hallucination pattern common in brands with strong television presence.

04
Listings (Q8) — ChatGPT cites 32m² apartment at €710k as Kretz example
Medium

ChatGPT cites a 32m² apartment priced at €710,000 as a representative Kretz listing. This is non-representative of the agency's ultra-luxury positioning: the average transaction ticket is €1.9M and the record sale is €33.85M. Citing a sub-€1M micro-apartment as a Kretz example fundamentally misrepresents the brand's market tier in agentic search outputs.

05
Netflix attribution (Q9) — Gemini and Perplexity contradict on €33.85M record sale
Medium

Gemini Pro claims the €33.85M Faubourg Saint-Honoré sale was filmed and broadcast in Seasons 4 & 5 of Netflix's "L'Agence." Perplexity correctly identifies this sale as covered via TF1 press coverage, not a Netflix episode. This contradiction between two major LLMs creates unreliable brand narrative around Kretz's most significant transaction — a 490m² apartment at Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, Paris 8e, purchased by a US crypto entrepreneur in early 2025.
